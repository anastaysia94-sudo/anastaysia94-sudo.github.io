About SmartPickShop Credentials

SmartPickShop Holdings uses project-based professional certificates to document demonstrated applied competencies. The credentials in this registry are issued internally by SmartPickShop Holdings and are based on documented project work, implementation, testing, research, operations, and/or deliverables relevant to the credential.

Transparency notice: These are internal project-based professional certificates. They are not represented as university degrees, professional licenses, government certifications, board certifications, or third-party accreditation.
